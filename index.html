Manual Company Entry

Quick guide for manual input

Add one company at a time here. This is the fastest way to test new companies without editing a CSV first.

  • company_name: the exact display name you want returned in results
  • mapped_function: the main branch the company belongs to
  • mapped_industry: the industry branch, or use General Business
  • match_keywords: verbs and nouns only, comma-separated
  • short_description: one plain-English sentence
  • product_description: a fuller explanation of what the product does
Good match_keywords: hiring, recruiting, screening, candidates, suppliers, sourcing, audit, anomalies, reporting, planning Avoid: smart, innovative, powerful, seamless, advanced, intelligent
Use verbs and nouns only. Separate with commas.
When the server is live, companies are embedded and indexed immediately — they'll appear in cosine similarity results right away and survive dyno restarts.
